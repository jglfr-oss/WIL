A COMIC FOR HUDSONThe Gold Wolf
From Dad.
ONE
This is Hudson's pack. Mom, Dad, family. The ones who would come looking for him no matter what.
One of them was gold. The only gold wolf for miles.
You're thirteen. Old enough to hear this straight. My father told me. Now it's your turn.
You are a wolf of this pack. Nothing changes that. Not where you go. Not who you run with.
TWO
Across the ridge lived another pack. Hudson had friends there. Real friends. Good ones.
Go. Run with them. Have fun. I'm not telling you not to.
But out there, you are the only gold wolf on that hill. And everyone can see it. Even the ones who love you.
THREE
One night they all went down to the river.
We're heading back! Catch up later!
Nobody looked back. Not because they were cruel. Because when trouble comes, a pack counts its own first. Every pack. Always.
That's the moment, Hudson. Not the fun part. The part where everyone else heads home.
FOUR
So here is what a gold wolf does.

The gold wolf rules

  1. Your pack always knows where you are and who you're with. Location on, phone charged, no exceptions.
  2. If the plan changes, you text. Before it changes, not after.
  3. Never be the one left behind. No "catch up later." You leave when they leave, or you don't go.
  4. If it feels wrong, leave. You don't owe anyone a reason.
  5. If your body feels off — tired, dizzy, low — say it out loud. Don't tough it out to fit in.
  6. With adults or police: calm voice, hands where they can see them. Stay safe now, argue later with Dad next to you.
  7. Call home. Always. No trouble, no questions, until you're safe.
I'm not telling you this because I'm scared for you. I'm telling you because I'm proud of you.
Gold is hard to hide. So don't try. Just watch your back the way we'd watch it.
And he went back over the ridge. He always went back. He just went smart.
THE END

Talk about it with Dad
